# GUIDE TITLE: verified facts and sources

Skeleton for a guide's facts. Copy it to `data/sources/APP-SLUG/GUIDE-SLUG.md` and fill it in
before writing the page. Delete this paragraph in the copy.

Reference for `APP-SLUG/guides/GUIDE-SLUG/index.html`. Every factual claim on that page must
trace to an entry here. Change the page freely; change a fact only after re-reading its source,
and update the date checked.

Checked: DAY MONTH YEAR.

**How the quotes were taken.** Say how each source was read: opened in a browser, or through a
tool that returns the page text. If it was a tool, treat each quote as checked wording rather
than a guaranteed byte-for-byte copy, and re-open the source before quoting it anywhere new.

## Our reasoning, not a source

Every line on the page that is advice or inference rather than a sourced fact, so nobody
mistakes it for one. Give the fact it rests on.

- "The sentence as it appears on the page." Rests on F1; the advice is ours.

## Not from a primary source

Anything taken from a comparison site, a directory, a provider's marketing, or memory. Say
which, and keep it off the page or hedge it there. Prices belong here: they change often, so
prefer no figure to a stale one.

## Left out on purpose

Claims that were considered and cut because no source supported them. Listing them stops the
next edit from putting them back.
