Privacy policy

For the Roadworthy Android app. Last updated 21 August 2026.

The short version
  • Photographs never leave your device. Number plates are read on the phone itself.
  • There is no server and no account. Nothing to log in to, and nothing stored about you remotely.
  • Vehicle lookups go directly from your device to DVLA's public service.
  • Advertising is separate, asks for your consent, and never sees vehicle data.

Roadworthy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the DVLA, DVSA, the Motor Insurers' Bureau, or any government body.

Your camera

Plate recognition runs entirely on your device using Google ML Kit. Images are never uploaded, never stored, and never sent anywhere. Only the registration you confirm is used, and only to perform a lookup.

Vehicle lookups

When you check a vehicle, your device contacts DVLA's public Vehicle Enquiry Service (vehicleenquiry.service.gov.uk) directly to retrieve tax and MOT status. The request comes from your device and your connection — it does not pass through us, because we operate no server.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests — providing the check you asked for

Vehicles you save

Saved vehicles are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to us and not synced. Deleting a vehicle, or the app, removes them.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, your device's position is converted to a street and town so a report can be completed; you can also type the address yourself and refuse the permission entirely.

Your location is used at that moment and is not stored or transmitted to us. It is included in a report only if you submit one.

Lawful basis: consent — you grant the permission, and the app works without it

Reporting a vehicle

If you choose to report a vehicle, the details you have reviewed on screen are submitted from your device to the relevant authority — DVLA for untaxed vehicles, or a police force. Nothing is submitted without you tapping submit, and we never submit on your behalf.

Those submissions are governed by the receiving authority's own privacy notice. DVLA's untaxed vehicle reports are anonymous to the vehicle keeper.

We are not told whether your report was accepted. It goes from your device to the authority, and no copy reaches us.

Insurance checks

Selecting "Check insurance" opens the Motor Insurers' Bureau's own service inside the app. We do not perform the check and do not see the result. You interact with MIB directly and their privacy notice applies. This is offered only for vehicles you have told us are yours, because MIB permit it only in that case.

Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob.

Advertising is a separate purpose from vehicle checking, and asks separately for your consent. In the UK and EEA you are shown a consent form before any advertising identifier is collected. If you refuse, the app works normally without ads.

Google may collect a device advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. See Google's own privacy policy for what they do with it.

Advertising never receives vehicle data. No registration, make, model, colour, location or report content is shared with advertisers or used for ad targeting. This is enforced in the code, not merely as a policy: the advertising component cannot access vehicle data, and an automated test fails the build if that separation is broken.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through Privacy options in the app's About screen.

Lawful basis: consent

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection.

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, most of these are exercised directly on your device: delete saved vehicles in the app, revoke permissions in Android settings, withdraw advertising consent through the app's privacy options, or uninstall.

For data held by DVLA, DVSA or MIB, contact them directly — they are the controllers of their own records.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Roadworthy is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the data flows change, this page changes with them and the date above is updated. The most likely such change is vehicle lookups moving to a server we operate, which would be described here before it ships.
