What it checks

What the full history check tells you

Data provided by Experian:

  • Whether a finance agreement is on record for the car.
  • Whether it's on the insurers' write-off register, and any category.
  • Whether it's recorded as stolen on the Police National Computer.
  • How many keepers it has had, and when it last changed hands.
  • Any change of number plate or colour.
  • Whether it's recorded as scrapped, exported or imported.

And free in Roadworthy, as for any car, from DVLA and DVSA: its tax and MOT, every MOT result with the mileage at each test, and every advisory.

Limits

What it can't tell you

  • It's a check of records. Something that was never recorded, or recorded wrongly, won't show.
  • It doesn't tell you the car's condition. Look it over, or have it inspected.
  • It doesn't tell you whether the seller is the keeper. Check the log book against the seller and the car.
  • It shows the records on the day you check. Check again if you buy later.
  • It can't show whether a car is a clone carrying another car's identity. Check the VIN on the car against the log book.

If part of the check doesn't come back

The report says "Not checked" for that part, never "None on record". If no report can be made, we refund you through Google Play.
